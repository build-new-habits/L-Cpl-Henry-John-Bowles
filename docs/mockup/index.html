Design mock-up

The Quiet Soldier

Eight static screens showing how the exhibit about L/Cpl Henry John Bowles could look and feel, in the order a visitor moves through it. Words in [square brackets] are placeholders until the research fills them in, and the drawings stand in for real scans, maps and photos.

These are fixed-size design screens (1280 × 800), so on a phone you will need to scroll sideways. The finished site will be fully responsive and keyboard operable.
